# Rule: Finnish accuracy (applies to content/ and lib/ data)

When editing anything under `content/` (words, weeks, reading texts) or data in `lib/`:

- This is public teaching material. **A single wrong ending misleads learners and
  embarrasses the owner. Treat accuracy as the top priority — above speed.**
- **Never invent or guess a Finnish form.** Verify non-trivial paradigms (consonant
  gradation, stem changes, irregular comparison, `-ida` verbs, contracted stems) against
  **Kielitoimiston sanakirja** and **Wiktionary** before saving.
- The wordmastery.org source list has duplicates and errors — do not copy it blindly.
  See `docs/FINNISH-ACCURACY.md` for the full protocol and the catalogue of known errors.
- Use the **finnish-word-entry** skill for the schema and per-entry checklist.
- Every item carries an accurate `updatedAt`.

If you are not certain a form is correct: stop and verify. Do not commit uncertain content.
